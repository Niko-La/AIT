    How it works

    Camera

    Pose detection runs on your phone; the video never leaves it. A hang starts when both elbows are above your shoulders and ends when your arms come down. If the bar is high, it's fine if your hands are out of frame.

    Pocket

    Standing and hanging feel the same to a phone in your pocket, so this mode times you from the moment you catch the bar to the moment you let go. Letting go is free fall with no push first, which you can't do from standing.

    • Jump up to the bar for the most accurate start.
    • Your feet must leave the ground. Just letting go and dropping a few inches is enough.
    • If you reach up without jumping, the hang counts from when you stopped moving.

    Armband

    When you tap Start, let that arm hang relaxed for 3 seconds while it calibrates. Any time the arm is raised overhead (rotated more than 120°) counts as hanging.

    Tips

    • The screen stays on while a session runs. If you lock the phone, timing pauses.
    • Switch off silent mode to hear beeps and spoken times.
    • Add to Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) to open it like an app. It works offline after the first visit.

    Apple Watch

    Websites can't run on Apple Watch, so there's a small native watch app in this repo's deadhang/watch folder. It times hangs from your wrist position, and you install it with Xcode.

    Tip: tap Share → Add to Home Screen to use this like an app.
